# Healthcare Operations Team Concepts

Mentor-ready DSV 2026 presentation comparing three healthcare operations problems:

1. Prior authorization preparation and coordination
2. Claims denial triage and appeal preparation
3. Hospital discharge handoff completion

## Status

These are documented problems and bounded product hypotheses for team discussion. Product demand, willingness to pay, differentiation, and agentic-AI necessity have not been validated.

The current recommendation is to validate prior authorization preparation first through a small payer-policy comparison and conversations with 3–5 prior-authorization specialists.
